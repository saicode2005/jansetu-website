Connecting surplus food with people and animals in need. Jansetu is a social-impact initiative that collects safe surplus food from restaurants, supermarkets, hotels and events and redistributes it to vulnerable communities through local partners.

    Questions

    Before you join

    What food can I donate?

    Safe food that is still good to eat: cooked meals, fresh vegetables and fruit, bakery items, packaged food, dairy and event leftovers. Partners can turn down anything that does not look safe.

    How does a donation reach people?

    A donor posts the food with a pickup time. An NGO or community group claims it, and a volunteer can accept the pickup. Everyone can follow the donation from collection to delivery on the tracking view.

    Who can join?

    Restaurants, supermarkets, hotels, caterers and individuals can donate. NGOs and community groups can receive food. Anyone can volunteer to collect and deliver.

    Does it cost anything?

    This prototype is free to try. Jansetu is a student venture concept, so real activities and partnerships will be added as the project grows.

    Is my account information safe?

    Accounts in this prototype are saved only in your own browser and are not sent anywhere. Please do not use a password you use on other sites.
